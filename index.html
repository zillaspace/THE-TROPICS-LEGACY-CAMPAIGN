Confidential Campaign Concept

The Tropics Legacy Campaign

Preserve a Palm Springs icon. Invite the community in. Turn investors and donors into lifelong ambassadors.

This is the strategic opportunity.
The campaign raises capital without diluting ownership and creates emotional ownership inside the community and investor base. A landmark hospitality property is not funded only by return math. It is funded by memory, recognition, belonging, and the chance to put a family name on a place people already love.

The property is not simply a hotel. It is one of Palm Springs remaining authentic Polynesian and tiki landmarks. It began as The Tropics, opened in nineteen sixty four by motel developer Ken Kimes, and its story touches mid century Palm Springs, Rat Pack era mythology, resort preservation, and the revival of tiki culture.

New Tiki Production Strategy

United States Master Carvers

Best for authenticity and provenance. Consider Bosko Hrnjak, Crazy Al Evans, Gecko, and John Mulder. Typical range is $3,000 to $8,000 for smaller work, $10,000 to $20,000 for medium pieces, and $25,000 to $75,000 or more for monumental work.

Collector phrase: Carved by Bosko for Caliente Tropics.

Indonesia

Most economical for volume. Bali and Java can produce resort scale carved work. Rough landed cost can be under $5,000 for many large pieces before special finishing, container variables, and installation.

Fiji

Best story if there is a real relationship there. The Tropics Ancestors Project could commission Fijian master carvers and give each donor a certificate of origin, photos of the carving process, and a dedicated plaque.

Wellness naming can attract donors outside the traditional hotel investor pool. A respected physician, health founder, therapist, nutrition leader, or wellness philanthropist is not just funding a hotel space. They are attaching their lifes work to a public facing legacy.

Recommended Campaign Architecture

Layer One

Historic Tiki Steward program for the original collection. Twenty four opportunities in the working model. $25,000 to $100,000 each.

Layer Two

Named districts for the strongest donors. Wellness, Pool Club, Gardens, Event Lawn, Main Bar, and Arrival Courtyard. $250,000 to $1,000,000 plus.

Layer Three

New Legacy Tiki Collection with twenty five to fifty new carvings that can carry donor names more directly. $25,000 to $100,000 each.

Final recommendation: keep the original Ed Crissman and Oceanic Arts linked tikis as the Historic Tropics Collection. Use stewardship plaques for those. Put donor names more boldly on new carvings and named resort districts.
